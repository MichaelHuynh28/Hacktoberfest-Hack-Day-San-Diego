# Star Scout


> *For the nurturing of curiosity, a wonder which extends as far as the stars in the galaxy.*

---


## Abstract
Uses a visual map to show the connection of each star in relation to the rest of the stars in the sky. By making our map a view from earth as it would be seen by a person, it creates a tangible visual which can be referenced and used when stargazing, allowing for the exploration of curiosity to bloom. 

---


## The Problem
Children and adults alike develop curiosities for the cosmos at different stages in their life, and it is as vast and overwhelming as it gets when it comes to learning all there is to know about something. So much so that it is deeply unknown to experts now and probably forever. Still, our curiosity continues. As each generation's technology updates and society advances, more and more information is asked to be absorbed from us, and so easier and better ways to do this are required. 


---

## The Solution
In order to get an introduction to the night sky's skeleton, we have created Star Scout. Allowing easy and fun navigation of the night sky's most beautiful and iconic stars. It rewards curiosity and reinforces the visual map with deep dive information and fun facts. 


---

## Key Features
* **Kid Mode:** Reduces the explanations to bullet points and easier to digest wording, allowing for access to all ages, and quicker surface level answers.
* **Quests:** Provides a fun scavenger hunt system in search for individual stars as they build up bigger constellations. It also rewards users with badges which represent the quests completed. Once a constellation is completed the whole constellation will glow to show its full image. 
* **Starlookup:** Allows for quick search of information along preselected fun facts displayed upon search.

* Finding a balance in audiences, targeting older and younger audiences.
* Working with API's for the first time.
* Communication in a team, bridging the gap of ideas and perspectives
* Deciding what challenges to attempt, and what product to build. With little restrictions on what to make, and lack of structure create an overwhelming set of possibilities when deciding on a project.
* Logistical practices and maintaining technical habits
